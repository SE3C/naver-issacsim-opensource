## Decision summary

The three checkpoints are not interchangeable aliases for one policy. They
represent different points and, for 4900, a different run segment:

| Candidate | Canonical identity | Role | Strongest evidence | Main limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fresh `model_4300.pt` | SHA-256 `82efb1a59bfad74c5b03b7584fe7cf27865dcd0603550f11340c4134c6a802c8` | reward-optimum stability control | original reward peak region; low nearby reward variance; finite tensors | lower curriculum exposure than 4800; no candidate-specific policy/video evaluation |
| fresh `model_4800.pt` | SHA-256 `ea93f9b5b04d91a91d1cdb97c6d328978017991e0602745f9269c7190bfaba65` | hard-terrain capability control and recovery source | terrain `5.7367`, XY error `0.5017`; cryptographic parent of recovery | high nearby reward variance, highest action std and larger motion costs; no matching exported policy in the initial comparison |
| recovery `model_4900.pt` | SHA-256 `250e430cbb74f837af15384c145e865f7c8d8155fc65a7fe8b21dda1c27514cd` | artifact-complete tracking challenger | evidence/finalize/backup PASS; `model_best` and `policy.pt` actor match; best seed-4201 mean XY RMSE | selected under easier post-resume curriculum; seed-4201 boxes/stairs fall rate exceeded 4800 |

Current decision after the first fixed-condition batch: promote original 4800
as the **multi-seed priority candidate**, not the final winner. In seed 4201,
4800 had the best six-terrain survival average (`98.18%`), worst-terrain
survival (`93.75%`), base-contact rate (`1.82%`), yaw RMSE, and action-rate
metric. Recovery 4900 had the best mean XY RMSE and displacement, but fell more
often on boxes and stairs. More seeds and checkpoint-matched exports/videos are
still required. 4300 remains the clean fresh-run control.

The subsequent six native-play videos for original 4800 and recovery 4900 were all
validated and support their use as a qualitative sanity check only. They did not fix the
controlled evaluator's command, curriculum state, difficulty, or traversal alignment, so
they do not change this selection boundary. See
`2026-09-15_GO2_G-005_4800_4900_NATIVE_VIDEO_AND_SELECTION.md`.

## Training evidence comparison

| Metric at saved iteration | 4300 | original 4800 | recovery 4900 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| mean reward | 20.65 | 20.14 | 24.40 |
| terrain level | 5.5469 | 5.7367 | 4.6251 |
| XY error | 0.5636 | 0.5017 | 0.5489 |
| yaw error | 0.3999 | 0.4278 | 0.3741 |
| episode length | 951.37 | 968.01 | 982.37 |
| base contact | 7.86% | 8.11% | 3.82% |
| timeout | 92.14% | 91.89% | 96.18% |
| action std | 0.64 | 0.66 | 0.57 |
| value loss | 0.1777 | 0.0259 | 0.0201 |

These point values are not a fair leaderboard. Recovery reset environment-side
curriculum statistics: terrain began again near `3.5275`, while original 4800
was at `5.7367`. The 4900 reward and survival metrics therefore include an
easier recent terrain distribution. Conversely, the recovery trajectory later
reached terrain `5.7940` at logged iteration 4999 without persistent collapse,
which is useful indirect evidence but not a fixed evaluation of checkpoint
4900 itself.

Window behavior distinguishes the candidates:

- 4300's preceding 20 iterations had reward std about `0.68`; the surrounding
  50–100 iteration region was a relatively clean high-reward segment.
- 4800's preceding 20 iterations had reward std about `6.80`, and the original
  long run repeatedly experienced large critic spikes even though metrics and
  checkpoints recovered and remained finite.
- 4900's preceding 50 recovery iterations had reward mean about `24.57`, std
  `0.79`, base contact about `3.62%`, and value-loss max `0.1777`; this is the
  cleanest local training segment but is confounded by curriculum reset.

## First fixed comparison received

The private L40S seed-4201, difficulty-1.0 batch evaluated all 18 combinations
of three checkpoints and six terrains with 64 environments each. Its archive
and all 45 internally listed files passed SHA-256 verification; every raw CSV
had 64 finite rows and reproduced its JSON summary.

The result does not establish an official score or final submission winner.
`success_timeout_pct` is only the fraction reaching the 20-second time limit
without `base_contact`, not proof of terrain traversal. All three checkpoints
barely progressed on inverted stairs despite 95–100% timeout survival. See
`2026-09-15_GO2_G-005_FIXED_EVAL_SEED4201_D1.md` for the full table and caveats.

## Remaining fixed comparison

Repeat the identical evaluator version, terrain, commands, horizon, and metric
aggregation for 4300, original 4800, and recovery 4900 with additional seeds.
At minimum compare:

- six-terrain average and worst-terrain success;
- stairs, inverted stairs, boxes, random rough, exact +20° slope, and exact
  -20° slope separately;
- fall/base-contact rate, timeout/survival, XY and yaw tracking error;
- seed-to-seed variance and push/recovery behavior;
- policy/checkpoint/source SHA and a video evidence manifest for every run.

Do not use the existing recovery 6-video handoff as a three-model comparison:
it contains only the recovery policy's videos and lacks individual video
evidence manifests.
