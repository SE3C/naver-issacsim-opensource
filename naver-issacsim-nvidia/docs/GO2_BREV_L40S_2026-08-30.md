## Video replay

Use scripts/record_go2_brev_l40s.sh for the preserved 5k and 9.5k policies.
It reproduces the successful run:

- one Go2 robot;
- 6,000 50 Hz steps (120 seconds of simulation time);
- headless camera capture;
- playback-only 1x1 terrain generation.

The 1x1 terrain override is only for video memory control. It does not alter the
training checkpoint or training configuration. On the 70 GiB CPU-RAM instance,
each 120-second replay took roughly 7–8 minutes wall-clock and peaked near 53 GiB
RAM, so run exactly one replay at a time.
