## Result and decision

After curriculum reacquisition, last-500 terrain was 5.372 and base-contact termination was
30.62%. XY error improved to 0.3386, but yaw error rose to 2.008 and smoothness-related
penalties worsened. The original native-video Release validates only early `model_1700`.
A later user-supplied six-terrain video collection is identified as final `model_2998`; its
MP4 hashes and media format are verified, but its checkpoint linkage is video-only provenance
without embedded checkpoint hash or command/runtime metadata.

Decision: **HOLD / 재실험 필요**. Historical values remain descriptive relative-comparison evidence. Rerun from the new +0.2 parent lineage with its unchanged control before adoption. H-007/008 additionally changed feet, so no track-only conclusion is supported. Final numbered checkpoints identify historical representative policies; they do not authorize final reward adoption.
